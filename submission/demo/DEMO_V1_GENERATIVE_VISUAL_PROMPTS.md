# SkinExo-AI DEMO-V1 generative visual prompt pack

These prompts specify optional production plates for DEMO-V1-D2. They are not requests to generate assets during D1. All scientific labels and values must be added later as project-owned typography, never generated inside an image.

## GV01 — Biological skin membrane opening

**Filename:** `gv01_biological_skin_membrane_16x9.png`
**Use:** Scene 1, KF01
**Aspect ratio:** 16:9, 3840 × 2160 preferred

**Composition**

An abstract macro view of luminous human skin microstructure and a translucent biological membrane, occupying the lower-right third. The upper-left and center remain clean Pearl White negative space for the SkinExo Lab and SkinExo-AI titles. No identifiable person or full face.

**Visual style**

Premium clinical editorial photography blended with restrained scientific visualization. Soft, diffuse light; realistic material depth; calm Korean medical-aesthetic sensibility; no cosmetic campaign styling.

**Palette and materials**

Pearl White, Warm Ivory, faint Soft Blush, Deep Ink only in later typography. Pearl, translucent membrane, soft liquid light, very subtle Champagne refraction.

**Negative constraints**

No face, eye, lips, makeup, cosmetics, bottle, syringe, injection, before/after effect, wound, lesion, logo, watermark, text, neon, chrome, cyberpunk, HUD, bloom overload, or beauty-advertising pose.

**Scientific constraints**

The plate is contextual atmosphere, not a histology image and not evidence. Do not depict treatment improvement, rejuvenation, healing, or efficacy.

**Animation-safe composition**

Separate foreground membrane, middle soft tissue texture, and background light gradients. Keep 15% clean margins and avoid fine details that shimmer under a 3% parallax move.

## Generation acceptance gate

Before any generated plate enters the edit:

1. inspect for accidental text, watermark, identifiable person, logo, product, or efficacy cue;
2. compare the palette with `SKINEXO_VISUAL_BIBLE.md`;
3. confirm the image is used only as a conceptual background;
4. record the generation method and prompt provenance in the asset manifest; and
5. reject any plate that looks like advertising or claims a biological result.
