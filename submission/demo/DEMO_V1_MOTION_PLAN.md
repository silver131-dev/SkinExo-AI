# SkinExo-AI DEMO-V1 motion plan

## Global motion lock

- Standard easing: `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`.
- Standard transition: 720 ms; slow scientific turn: 850–900 ms.
- Use soft dissolve, slow float, glass slide, gentle parallax, and liquid morph only.
- Honor title-safe and caption-safe zones throughout motion.
- No glitch, neon pulse, rapid zoom, camera shake, fast flash, spinning data, or HUD animation.
