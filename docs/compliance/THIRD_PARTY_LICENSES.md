| European Nucleotide Archive (ENA) | ENA metadata and sequence links remain governed by EMBL-EBI/ENA policies and source-study terms. Small public metadata snapshots are attributed to ENA. |
| MSigDB | Gene-set names and analysis provenance identify the frozen MSigDB release. GMT resources are not redistributed and remain subject to MSigDB licensing and collection-specific terms. |
| GENCODE | Annotation releases remain subject to GENCODE/Ensembl attribution and use terms. Large annotation source files are not redistributed. |
| Scientific publications | Article text, figures, supplements, and publisher files retain their copyrights and access conditions. Citations, DOIs, PubMed identifiers, and project-authored summaries do not relicense a publication. |
| Institutional-access literature | No institutional authentication material or licensed full text is part of the public snapshot. The licensed Wiley PDF used during authorized research review is excluded from Git and is not redistributed. |
| Third-party software | Python, R, Bioconductor, Streamlit, DESeq2, GSEApy, and other dependencies retain their respective licenses. Listing a dependency in this repository does not relicense it under MIT. |
